Option B won: the pill should be a light material on every site. These six recipes vary only the glass — opacity, blur radius, saturation, tint and edge. A city-cam cover sits behind the first selection so the blur has real colour and edges to diffuse; the second selection shows the same material over plain dark text. Each caption also shows the same busy underlay three ways — raw, tint only (no blur), and through glass — so the blur is impossible to miss.
